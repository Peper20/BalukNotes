# E3. WOFF2 with the `glyf` transform and a subset of the math font

**Questions** (tech debt "WOFF2 without the `glyf` transform", "The math font
is not subset"): (1) how much the `glyf`/`loca` transform from the WOFF2
specification gives and whether it can be done; (2) how much a subset of New
Computer Modern Math with the `MATH` table would give.

**Conclusion.** (1) -11.5 % for TrueType font parts, the same outlines -
**done**: `ttf2woff2` for Gentium Plus and JetBrains Mono
(`notes-core::webfonts`). (2) A math subset with the `MATH` closure would
shrink it from 669 KB to ~190 KB for an ordinary page, but there is no such
subsetter in Rust (`klippa` handles neither CFF nor `MATH`) - only a report
and an option in the tech debt.

## 1. The `glyf` transform

**Method.** All 48 TrueType font parts the `tests/vault` server compresses
for the browser (cache `tests/.data/cache/fonts`: 2 fonts x 4 styles x 6
parts) were re-encoded: fontTools 4.66 (`woff2`, with and without the
transform), then `ttf2woff2` 0.13.3 (brotli 11). Check: fontTools decodes the
new and the old part, compares outlines (`RecordingPen`) and advances of all
glyphs and the bytes of all other tables.

| Encoder | 48 parts, B | vs the old one |
|---|---|---|
| the old own one (no transforms) | 2 330 572 | - |
| fontTools without transforms | 2 329 924 | -0.03 % |
| fontTools with `glyf`/`loca` | 2 060 956 | -11.6 % |
| `ttf2woff2` (now in the core) | 2 061 828 | -11.5 % |

Outlines and advances: 0 differences; other tables (including `head`, `GPOS`,
`cmap`) - byte for byte. Page screenshots in the browser (light and dark
theme) before and after are pixel-identical.

What it gives a Russian page (Gentium: regular, italic, bold - Latin +
Cyrillic; JetBrains Mono regular - Latin + Cyrillic): 309 -> 281 KB (-28 KB,
-9 %). The math font (CFF, 669 KB) does not change - in total ~978 -> ~950 KB
of fonts (-3 %). (KB here is 1024 bytes.) Compression time - ~0.15 s per part
in release, once (disk cache).

The cost: `ttf2woff2` pulls brotli 9, and `tower-http` brotli 8: two copies of
brotli in the build (tech debt).

## 2. A math subset with `MATH`

**Method.** New Computer Modern Math Regular (typst-assets 0.15.1: 8603
glyphs, 4887 characters, OTF/CFF, WOFF2 whole - 669 KB) -> `fontTools.subset`
(all OpenType features, `MATH` with the closure: size variants and assembly
parts of stretchy glyphs) -> WOFF2.

| Character set | characters | glyphs after the closure | WOFF2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| whole | 4887 | 8603 | 669 KB |
| characters of all `tests/vault` formulas | 76 | 318 | 32 KB |
| basic: Latin, Greek, operators, arrows | 828 | 2052 | 156 KB |
| + italic variables (Latin, Greek) | 938 | 2385 | 192 KB |
| + upright bold, double-struck ℝ, script | 1094 | 2862 | 255 KB |
| everything "mathematical" (+ the blocks 2300-23FF, 27C0-2AFF, 1D400-1D7FF whole) | 2574 | 5343 | 483 KB |

An ordinary page would get by with the "basic + italic" part (~190 KB instead
of 669) and would download the rest as a `rest` part only on a rare glyph.

**Why it is not done:**
- nobody in Rust can subset CFF with the `MATH` closure: `fontcull`/`klippa`
  - only TrueType and without `MATH`; fontTools is Python;
- stretchy brackets and roots: a glyph variant must lie in the same part as
  the glyph itself (the closure gives that), and the shared `MATH` parameters
  (`MathConstants`) - in every part; how browsers (Chromium, WebKit, Gecko)
  handle MathML whose font is split by `unicode-range` has to be checked by
  eye in each.

**Options** (in the tech debt): math font parts cut by fontTools in advance
and kept in the repository (rebuild on a font or Typst change); or a TrueType
version of the math font (then `fontcull` subsets it, but without `MATH` -
that needs support in `klippa`).
